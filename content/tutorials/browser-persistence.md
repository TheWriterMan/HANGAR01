Browser Persistence has been a feature of HANGAR01 from the start
We have all been there. You are in the middle of a preflight setup or a long cruise phase, and your browser crashes, you accidentally close the tab, or you need to step away from your simulator setup. When you return, all of your carefully checked items are gone, and you have to start the checklist from scratch.

No more.

With our new **Browser Persistence** feature, HANGAR01 automatically saves your checklist progress in real-time as you complete each task.

## **How it works**

Every time you toggle a checkbox, HANGAR01 stores your state using the browser's local storage. Your progress is tied to the specific aircraft and checklist level you are using.

![Four steps: tick an item; it is saved at once for this aircraft and level; close the tab, refresh or restart; open the checklist again and your ticks are back](/images/news/browser-persistence/browser-how-cyan.webp)

- **No account required**: Everything is stored completely client-side. No log-ins, no passwords, and no tracking.
- **Instant updates**: Toggle an item, and it's saved instantly.
- **State restoration**: Refresh the page, close the browser, or restart your system. When you load the aircraft checklist again, your progress will be exactly where you left it.

## **Resetting your flight**

If you want to clear your progress and start a brand-new flight, simply scroll to the bottom of the page and click the **Reset Flight** button. This will clear the saved state for the active aircraft, allowing you to start fresh with a clean slate.

Safe flying!
