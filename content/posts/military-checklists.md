HANGAR01 now has a Military category. The first two aircraft are the Blackbird Simulations C-130J Super Hercules and the IndiaFoxtEcho F-35A Lightning II, both for MSFS. You'll find them under Aircraft – Military, or with the new filter on the aircraft page.

## C-130J Super Hercules

The Hercules first flew in 1954 and has stayed in production ever since. The J looks much like the originals from the outside, but almost everything else changed: four Rolls-Royce AE 2100 turboprops, six-blade composite propellers, a glass cockpit, and a flight deck that no longer needs a navigator or flight engineer.

Blackbird's version is MSFS 2024 only and reached 1.0 in March after a long stretch in early access. The team also builds simulators for real military customers, and it shows in how much of the aircraft actually works.

In the sim you're the pilot, copilot and crew chief at once, so the checklist puts all three flows into one path from power-up to shutdown. It runs to 88 items across eight phases, and Quick mode trims that to about 30. This first version covers normal procedures. Emergency, airdrop and air-to-air refuelling procedures aren't included, and emergency checklists are planned as their own section of the site.

## F-35A Lightning II

The A is the conventional takeoff and landing F-35, the variant the US Air Force flies. A single Pratt & Whitney F135 makes around 43,000 lb of thrust in afterburner, enough for Mach 1.6.

The F-35 was the first front-line fighter in 50 years to fly without a head-up display – flight and targeting symbology is projected onto the pilot's helmet visor instead. Nearly everything else lives on one wide touchscreen that spans the instrument panel. Start-up runs through the Integrated Power Package, a single unit that does the work an APU and an emergency power unit would do on older jets.

It has 101 items across seven phases, with about 35 in Quick mode, and goes straight from the climb to the approach. There's no shutdown phase yet.
