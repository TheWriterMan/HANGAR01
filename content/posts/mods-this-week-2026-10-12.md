FSimUK filled the weekend with announcements, from [iniBuilds' first business jet](https://msfsaddons.com/2026/10/10/inibuilds-trades-the-hub-for-the-fbo-with-its-first-business-jet-the-global-jet-for-msfs-2024/) to Miltech's Dash 8, but none of them can be installed yet. This recap covers what shipped between 5 and 11 October, led by an airliner family that spent almost four years in early access.

## Aircraft

### FlightSim Studio E-Jets 1.0

:::meta
FlightSim Studio
MSFS 2020, runs in 2024
€50.38 per pack plus VAT, free update for owners
:::

The E170 to E195 family [has reached version 1.0](https://flightsim-studio.com/news/e-jets-1.0-available) with a rebuilt autopilot, a rewritten MCDU and flight plan system, and Hoppie datalink for PDC, ATIS and CPDLC. Owners should do a clean install before flying it, and new buyers now pay [€50.38 instead of the €33.57 early-access price](https://msfsaddons.com/2026/10/05/the-fss-e-jets-are-finally-out-of-early-access-almost-four-years-after-launch/). A native MSFS 2024 version is next.

### Carenado CT206G Stationair

:::meta
Carenado
MSFS 2024
$22.99, Marketplace only
:::

The turbocharged 206 comes in passenger, cargo, skydiving, tundra and amphibian versions, with a round-gauge panel built around a GNS 530/430 and a KFC 225 autopilot. It works in Career Mode, and you can only buy it [on the MSFS Marketplace](https://www.flightsimulator.com/carenado-ct206/).

### PropairFlight Cheyenne III

:::meta
PropairFlight
MSFS 2020 and 2024
HUF 14,900 on PropairFlight's store
:::

The pressurised PA-42 twin turboprop [comes bundled with a SkyPass type-rating course](https://propairflight.com/products/ch3a) of about 50 hours, with AI-assisted debriefs after each mission. You choose between a G1000 and an analogue panel, and between passenger and medevac cabins.

### Skyward Simulations Citation Sovereign+

:::meta
Skyward Simulations
MSFS 2020 and 2024
$29.15 at Orbx
:::

Skyward's G5000 business jet, already out for MSFS 2024, now has a native MSFS 2020 build. That version keeps the walkaround features but uses a simpler electrical model, and [Orbx sells both builds as one product](https://orbxdirect.com/product/skyward-citation-msfs).

### FSReborn Phenom 300E v1.030

:::meta
FSReborn
MSFS 2024
update for owners
:::

The update adds CPDLC through the G3000 for VATSIM, IVAO, SayIntentions.AI and BeyondATC, a panel for triggering failures by hand, and crew profiles that remember your avionics pages between flights. [Threshold lists the full changelog](https://thresholdx.net/fsreborn-updates-phenom-300e-to-v1-030-adds-cpdlc-new-failures/).

## Scenery

### Doha Hamad International (OTHH)

:::meta
IGTEI
X-Plane 12
€28.00 plus VAT at simMarket
:::

IGTEI's first airport models all five concourses and 120 jetways matched to the real stands. The studio calls this first version an early-access offer: [buy it now and the pricier professional version is free](https://secure.simmarket.com/igtei-doha-hamad-intl-%28othh%29-xp12.phtml) when it arrives.

### Cleveland Burke Lakefront (KBKL)

:::meta
Dfflyer Direct
MSFS 2020 and 2024
€14.99 plus VAT at simMarket
:::

The downtown lakefront field has full interiors in every hangar, the terminal and the tower, and a snow-clearing fleet that comes out with the weather. [Contrail sells it with Dfflyer's Cleveland city scenery](https://contrail.shop/products/dfflyer-cleveland-ohio-kbkl-burke-lakefront-airport-bundle-msfs-2020-2024) for €18.99, and [simMarket sells the airport alone](https://secure.simmarket.com/kbkl-cleveland-burke-lakefront-airport.phtml).

### Houston William P. Hobby (KHOU)

:::meta
Fly2High
MSFS 2024
€17.99 plus VAT at simMarket
:::

Fly2High's Hobby has [interactive docking guidance at the gates](https://secure.simmarket.com/khou-william-p.-hobby-airport-msfs24.phtml) and animated screens throughout the terminal.

### Accurate France: Biomes France

:::meta
AEF-Flightsimulator
MSFS 2024
€19.08 plus VAT at simMarket
:::

This replaces the sim's generic forests with the tree species that actually grow in each part of France, built [from national map data across 2.72 million polygons](https://secure.simmarket.com/accurate-france-biomes-france-msfs24.phtml).

## Utilities and tools

### SayIntentions.AI Live Maps V2

:::meta
SayIntentions.AI
MSFS and X-Plane 12
included in the subscription
:::

The [rebuilt map](https://sayintentions.ai/blog?p=live-maps-v2) shows airport diagrams with your taxi route, weather overlays and smoother traffic, and lets you request a gate. It runs in the desktop client, the PocketSky phone app and the MSFS EFB.

### BeyondATC 1.8.22

:::meta
BeyondATC
MSFS
early-access update for owners
:::

BeyondATC no longer needs a separate helper to talk to the sim, so [airports load much faster](https://msfsaddons.com/2026/10/07/beyond-atc-gets-updated-with-faster-airport-loading-and-smoother-traffic/), and AI traffic moves more smoothly. It now warns you when a broken AIRAC update has dropped SIDs, STARs or approaches. The team also [confirmed official X-Plane support](https://fselite.net/content/beyondatc-announces-official-x-plane-support/), with no date yet.

### Navigraph Charts: Rogers Data VFR expansion

:::meta
Navigraph
Navigraph Charts
subscription
:::

Rogers Data VFR charts now [cover parts of North Africa and the Middle East](https://thresholdx.net/navigraph-with-rogers-data-vfr-expansion-new-chart-features/), and Croatia and Slovenia get charts at a finer scale than the previous 1:200,000.

## Updates to aircraft we have checklists for

### iniBuilds A380 Airliner v1.0.3

:::meta
iniBuilds
MSFS 2024
update for owners on the Marketplace
:::

The A380's second update, after v1.0.1 added opt-in crash reporting in September, also carries the v1.0.2 fixes that never shipped on their own. The fixes cover autobrakes going to MAX without input, unresponsive engines after GA SOFT and the body gear steering animation. A new EFB dropdown lets you choose British Airways or Emirates versions of the checklist, and one switch now turns off maintenance and persistence. [The full changelog is on the iniBuilds forum](https://forum.inibuilds.com/topic/41568-inibuilds-a380-airliner-for-microsoft-flight-simulator-2024-v1023-released/).

Our [A380 checklist](/aircraft/a380) is written for the FlyByWire A380X. The flows follow the same Airbus logic and mostly carry over, although panel layouts and EFB steps differ.

## Freeware pick of the week

### Fenix TechLog

:::meta
NaughtyPickle
MSFS 2024
free on flightsim.to, needs the Fenix A319, A320 or A321
:::

Unless you set failures yourself, every Fenix flight starts with a perfect aircraft. TechLog gives each airframe a memory instead: parts wear, defects carry over to the next flight, and a 207-item MEL decides whether you defer the fault or stay on the ground. It runs as a Windows app with an EFB page and a browser view for home cockpits, and [MSFS Addons explains how it works](https://msfsaddons.com/2026/10/09/this-free-add-on-brings-the-state-saving-fenix-a320-owners-have-been-asking-for/), and the download is [on flightsim.to](https://flightsim.to/addon/116154/fenix-techlog-persistent-tech-log-mel-and-live-failures-for-the-fenix-a319-a320-a321).
